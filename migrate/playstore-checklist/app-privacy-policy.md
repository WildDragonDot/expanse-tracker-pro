# Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker Pro

**Last updated:** September 2026

Welcome to **Expense Tracker Pro** ("we", "our", or "app"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Application:
- **Account Information:** Name, Email address, and Profile Picture (via Google Sign-In or direct registration).
- **Financial & Expense Data:** Daily expenses, income records, monthly budgets, debt records (Udhar), split group data, and notes entered by you.
- **Media / Receipts (Optional):** Photos or receipts you choose to upload to verify expenses.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for providing and improving the App features:
- To authenticate and maintain your account.
- To calculate monthly budgets, savings goals, and financial analytics.
- To sync your records across your devices using Google Firebase infrastructure.
- **We NEVER sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third-party advertisers.**

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

All your data is securely stored on **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Firebase Authentication)** servers with industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

---

## 4. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting us at `support@chandandev.online`.
